# InstaScanSpeak logo candidates

## b1 - Bubble Bites (flat bold geometric tile)
A dark rounded app tile holds a bold white speech bubble filled with four dark text lines while three teal sound bars emerge to its right.
At tray size the white bubble is a single strong mass against the dark tile, so it reads on both light and dark taskbars.

## b2 - Ink Screen (minimal monoline mark)
A light tile carries a single-weight dark outline of a screen with text lines and three sound bars beside it.
The even 26px strokes stay legible at 16x16 and the two masses (screen, wave) stay separate.

## b3 - Gloss Bubble (gradient glass bubble)
A glossy violet-to-cyan speech bubble with a glass highlight holds white text lines on the left and white sound bars on the right.
The saturated gradient bubble is one high-contrast mass that pops on either taskbar color.

## b4 - Radio Screen (retro vintage badge)
A cream and rust circular badge frames a dark retro screen full of mustard scan lines with three rust sound arcs broadcasting from the right.
The filled circle keeps a hard silhouette at small sizes while the warm palette reads clearly on both backgrounds.

## b5 - Blip (playful mascot screen)
A blue rounded-square character with a speech tail has two white eyes and a white soundwave mouth, plus pink cheeks.
The single friendly face mass is recognizable in one glance at 16x16, and the bright blue holds up on light and dark taskbars.
