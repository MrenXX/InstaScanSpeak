InstaSpeak logo pitch 2

Three iterations on Whisper Bubble, three on Bubble Bites. Every design tries a different way to say the same thing: text on a screen becomes your voice.

Whisper Bubble lineage

parent
Whisper A - Script to Sound
transparent outline, no tile

One shape, one idea: text lines on the left dissolve into voice bars on the right. The outline is quiet ink instead of a filled bubble, so it sits lighter and more editorial on any taskbar.

32px
Whisper B - Aurora Bubble
gradient fill, glossy

The bubble carries the gradient: midnight blue melting into amber, so day-to-night reading. Lines above, waveform below, one sheen of glass light to give it depth.

32px
Whisper C - Scanner Draft
paper + diagonal OCR laser

The scanner moved inside the bubble: ink text lines under a diagonal laser sweep, voice bars already waiting on the right. Laser color is borrowed from Whisper A3 so the two features chime.

32px

Bubble Bites lineage

parent
Bites A - Mirror Bite
mirrored composition, moss + lime

The parent flipped: tail and bars now strike left, text lines sit right-aligned. Tile drops near-black for moss green and the bars go lime, so it reads as a sibling, not a recolor.

32px
Bites B - Split Screen
page and voice split the tile

No bubble at all: the tile itself splits into the two halves of the product. A bright page with scanned lines on the left, dark sound side with teal bars on the right, one slanted seam between them.

32px
Bites C - Violet Radio
round tile, violet + amber

The only round tile of both pitches. Deep violet with a quiet inner ring, white bubble, amber bars. A warmer, rounder cousin if the dark chrome of the others bores you.

32px

All six are standalone SVGs in artifacts/logo/. Say a letter and it becomes the real tray icon.
